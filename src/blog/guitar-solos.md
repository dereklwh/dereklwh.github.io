# My Favourite Guitar Solos

I love a good guitar solo. There is something so satisfying about a song building itself up and then handing the whole thing over to the guitar for a while, and it is even better live because you get to watch it happen in front of you with no second chances. Here are some guitar solos that I love:

1. [Hotel California](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09839DpTctU) by the Eagles. The ending where the two guitars go back and forth and eventually play together is probably my favourite two minutes in any song.
2. [Nothing Else Matters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WIi6Upq5p58) by Metallica. The solo is slow and melodic, which was not really what I expected from Metallica when I first heard it.
3. [海闊天空](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V4GUy2EHMMs) by BEYOND. 家駒 wrote this back in 1993 and it is still everywhere today. The guitar solo gets me every time.
4. [Run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K09_5IsgGe8) by Joji. I was not expecting a full electric guitar solo in a Joji song and it completely takes over the track.

These are the ones that stuck out to me but there are many more that I am surely forgetting. If you have any recommendations for good guitar solos, let me know. Thanks!
